What is being demonstrated?

The arena is an original, simplified Pokémon battle simulator. Damage is deterministic; the harness supplies matchups and action forecasts. The model chooses actions. A win here does not prove it can complete a Pokémon game. The separate PyBoy adapter requires your own ROM and matching symbols. No AWS account is needed for local inference.
